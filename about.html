ABOUT

Fabian Pitzer considers code playful material and uses it to build custom graphic design tools. He aims to make space for the human in digital environments. He has a BA in Interactive Media from the UAS Augsburg and the KABK Den Haag and graduated with a MA in Visual Communication from the ZHdK. He is an alumni of the German Academic Scholarship Foundation. Following an Internship at Studio Moniker, he worked as a Creative Technologist for Lab Binær and now works part time as a programming tutor for kids. He is available for work surrounding graphic design, interaction, code and the web.
